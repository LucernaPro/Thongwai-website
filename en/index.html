Why Thongwai

Stream, mountains and cool air all year

Right on the stream

A natural stream runs through the resort — swim, kayak, or dine by the water surrounded by its sound

Bolaven Plateau

Paksong sits over 1,000 m above sea level — comfortably cool all year with no air-conditioning needed

True Lao coffee, by the stream

Enjoy coffee grown on the Bolaven Plateau at our riverside corner, with food and drinks served all day

Getting here & contact

Getting to Thongwai

Thongwai Homestay is in Ban Thongwai, Paksong, Champasak, Laos — on the southern-Laos / Bolaven Plateau route, near Paksong’s famous waterfall circuit

By car: cross at the Chong Mek–Vang Tao border, pass through Pakse and head for Paksong. Contact us on any channel for detailed directions or trip tips
