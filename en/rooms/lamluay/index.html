Lam Luay House

🛏 1 bedroom 🚿 1 bathroom 👥 Sleeps 2 ➕ Extra bed available 🌿 Front terrace 🏡 By the resort entrance
One-bedroom house

One bedroom, one bathroom, for 2 guests

Lam Luay House is a one-bedroom, one-bathroom house sleeping 2 (extra bed available), set by the resort entrance — a small house to yourselves, suited to couples.

The bedroom has dark woven-bamboo walls and a wooden floor, with a large bed and timber headboard, full bedding, a traditional-pattern runner and cushions, white cotton curtains, and a framed picture of coffee cherries beside the bed.

The front of the house is a timber terrace with rattan chairs, a round table and a rattan lantern overhead, looking out to the resort forecourt and the trees.

The en-suite bathroom is finished in bamboo poles and white tile with wood-effect floor tiles, and has a water heater with a shower, a mirror, and wall-mounted soap and shampoo.
