Mang Mee Ngoen Thong House

👥 Sleeps 2 ➕ Extra bed available 🌿 Front terrace 🏕 Camping-view zone
A small house

A small house to yourselves, in the camping-view zone

Mang Mee Ngoen Thong House is a small house sleeping 2 (extra bed available), set in the camping-view zone, with a terrace out front.

The bedroom has woven-bamboo walls and ceiling over a wooden floor, with two beds side by side, full bedding, cushions and a traditional bed runner, plus a wooden bedside table and framed pictures on the walls.

The window has a white roman blind, with full-length curtains along the other side, so you can adjust the light as you like.
