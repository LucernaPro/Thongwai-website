Chok Lap Ngoen Thong House

🛏 2 bedrooms 🚻 2 bathrooms 👥 Sleeps 4 ➕ Extra bed available 🌊 Riverside zone 🪑 Shared living area
Two-bedroom house, riverside zone

Two bedrooms, two bathrooms and a shared living area

Chok Lap Ngoen Thong House is a two-bedroom, two-bathroom house that sleeps 4 (extra bed available), set in the riverside zone beside the waterside terrace.

The bedrooms are finished with woven-bamboo walls and wooden floors — beds with full bedding, a traditional-pattern runner and cushions, plus a bedside cabinet and cotton curtains at every window.

At the centre of the house is a shared living area with a wooden table and four chairs, a counter with a sink and a kettle, for relaxing together indoors.
