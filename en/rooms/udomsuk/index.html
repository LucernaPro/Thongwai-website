Udom Suk House

🛏 2 bedrooms 🚻 2 bathrooms 🏠 Central hall 👥 Sleeps 2 per bedroom ➕ Extra bed available 🏕 Camping-view zone
Two-bedroom house

Two bedrooms, a central hall, and a lawn out front

Udom Suk House has two bedrooms, each sleeping 2 (extra bed available), with 2 bathrooms and a central hall for relaxing together.

Bedrooms are finished with woven-bamboo walls and wooden floors — a large bed with full bedding and a traditional-pattern runner, plus a wooden table and drinking water in the room.

The front of the house opens onto the wide camping-view lawn, looking out to the thatched pavilions, the campfire circle and the Bolaven hills.
